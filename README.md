# DCUGen Hero Forge

A character generator and GM toolkit for the **DC Adventures RPG** (Mutants & Masterminds 3e rules).

**Download [`DCUGen.html`](https://github.com/Beherrit/DCUGen/releases/latest/download/DCUGen.html) and open it in any modern browser.** It is one self-contained file: send it to your
players and they can use it too. No install, works offline (fonts fall back to system fonts without internet).

**Or install the desktop app:** [Windows installer](https://github.com/Beherrit/DCUGen/releases/latest/download/DCUGen-Setup.exe) ·
[Windows portable](https://github.com/Beherrit/DCUGen/releases/latest/download/DCUGen-Portable.exe) ·
[macOS](https://github.com/Beherrit/DCUGen/releases/latest/download/DCUGen.dmg) ·
[Linux](https://github.com/Beherrit/DCUGen/releases/latest/download/DCUGen.AppImage) ·
[all releases](https://github.com/Beherrit/DCUGen/releases). GitHub builds a new release from every push to `main`, and
installed apps update themselves. Your data lives in a folder on your own disk, and you can host a table for your players.

**On a phone or tablet:** open [forgedcugen.vercel.app](https://forgedcugen.vercel.app/) and add it to your home screen
(iPhone: Share > Add to Home Screen; Android: ⋮ > Install app). It opens full screen with its own icon and works
offline. The site's **📲 App** button (phones) and the **▾** next to Download app (computers) show the steps and QR codes:
one to open DCUGen on your phone, one for each desktop download.

## What's inside

- **Help** – the **?** at the top: a short tour for players and one for GMs (offered on a first visit), What's new
  after an update, and the version you're on.
- **Forge** – roll a complete, legal character in one click. Pick power level, archetype (20 including androids
  and combat robots), power theme (29), hero or villain, gender and how wild the dice should be, or hit
  *Surprise me*. Every character obeys every power level limit and spends exactly 15 points per PL. Seeds make
  rolls repeatable. *New name* keeps the build; *New build* keeps the name and story (both under **More ▾**, with
  Range, Share / Key, the World page, companions and forms). *Roll a team* makes a squad. On a phone the roll options
  fold into one bar once a character is open, so the sheet comes first.
- **Edit** – change any trait with +/−. Costs and PL limits update live, and problems are listed.
- **Power Lab** – build custom powers from all 61 DC Adventures effects with their extras and flaws, with the point
  math shown step by step. Random powers only use modifiers that fit the effect. Give any effect descriptors and your
  own description (how it looks, what it can do), which shows on the sheet and in the stat block. Build arrays of
  Alternate Effects (1 point each), switch on Dynamic to share the primary's points as a pool (2 points each), and add
  Linked effects that work together with one action (full cost each).
  Tick **Pool, not always on** for drains, stolen or redistributed points and boosts: the power counts for nothing
  until you place its points during play with + and − on the sheet (on any ability, defense, skill, power, or
  something else with a note). Pools count in power points the way Weaken drains them (DCA 122): an ability rank
  costs 2, a defense rank 1, two skill ranks 1, a power rank its cost per rank, so Enhanced Stamina 6 is a 12-point
  pool. Pick a Weaken under **Filled by** and the pool holds only what that Weaken has drained: every Weaken has a
  **Drain** tracker on the sheet (the target missed by N, −1 as points fade, all back), and placed ranks fall off
  newest first as the drained points go. PL limits are checked on the build without placed points; placed points
  that push a defense pair over the limit show as a warning.
- **Alternate forms** – More ▾ > 🎭 Alternate forms on the Forge gives a character up to 8 forms (civilian and costumed, armor on and
  off, a shapeshifter's shapes). Each form is a whole build with its own PL, traits, powers, gear, codename and
  picture, checked on its own; the person, bio, journal, world and roster entry are shared. Switch with the numbered
  tabs; the forms travel with the character in the roster, exports and keys.
- **Companions** – More ▾ > 🐾 Add a companion (or + Companion under the sheet) adds a sidekick (5 power points per rank of Sidekick, DCA 78), a minion (15 per rank
  of Minion, minion rules, DCA 77) or any other follower (an animal, a summoned creature, an ally), each with a full
  character sheet: roll one, start blank, take a creature from the Bestiary or copy someone from the roster. The
  hero's advantage is added for you and the budget is checked; edit the companion right on the hero's sheet or open
  it in its own tab and save it back.
- **Workshop**
  - *Gear loadouts* for an equipment-point budget, in styles from street tough to sci-fi.
  - *Gadgets and magic items*: named devices with real powers, priced with the Removable rule, legal at your PL.
  - *Minion squads*: thugs, soldiers, ninjas, cultists, agents, henchmen, alien troopers, robot drones and zombies,
    with what it costs a villain to field them (Minion advantage or Summon).
  - *Robots*: androids and combat robots built as constructs (no Stamina, immune to Fortitude effects).
  - *Animals and creatures*: the DC Adventures animals plus 142 stat blocks of everyday animals, wild beasts,
    monsters and people, with templates (young, alpha, dire, giant, mutant, cybernetic, undead, spectral).
  - *Monster maker*: new monsters built to any PL.
- **Bestiary** – 429 stat blocks, all legal for their PL: fantasy (dragons, giants, fey, elementals, dungeon
  aberrations), horror and undead, world myth and cryptids, aliens and cosmic entities, robots, mechs, clockwork and
  golems, kaiju, everyday animals and people. Each has tactics, habitat and a weakness; open it as a full sheet, apply
  a template, save it, or throw a random encounter into initiative.
- **Vehicles & HQs** (in Gear & Vehicles) – 107 vehicles (modern, military, sci-fi starships, fantasy galleons and sky-ships, steampunk) and
  51 headquarters, all priced with the DC Adventures equipment rules (DCA 155-163), plus the vehicle, spaceship and
  headquarters rules. Hand any of them to the open character; the Equipment advantage updates itself.
- **Bio** – every rolled character gets a full life story: family and siblings, a life timeline, the people in their
  life (mentors, rivals, enemies, exes), personality, secrets, regrets, hopes, GM hooks, and complications you can add
  to the sheet. Re-roll any part. The tables are original, inspired by the structure of Central Casting: Heroes Now!
- **Character keys** – every rolled character has a short key (Share / Key) that rebuilds it exactly, bio and all,
  in anyone's copy of the app: paste it into "Open a key" on the Forge. Every name in a bio is clickable and opens
  that person (parent, sibling, rival, mentor...) as a full character with their own bio, stats that fit who they
  are, and a link back. A character that has been edited or played since it was rolled (or rolled with an older
  version of the tables) gets a longer key that carries its seed plus everything that changed, journal included; only
  characters with no seed at all (built by hand, imported from a sheet, catalog creatures) use the full share code.
- **Gear & Vehicles** – 712 items across 18 genres (modern, military, espionage, fantasy, magic items, sci-fi, space,
  steampunk, wild west, pulp, cyberpunk, post-apocalyptic, occult, superhero gadgets...), a custom item maker, 169
  vehicles including 80 starships, headquarters, a builder for your own vehicles and bases, and space-game rules:
  travel times, sensors, ship combat, hazards and a random planet generator.
- **Power points from the GM** – a points bar at the top of every sheet: what the character is built on, what the
  GM has awarded, what's spent and what's **saved**. "🎁 Points from the GM" adds an award (every alternate form gets
  it), "Spend" opens edit mode and logs what you buy, and points you don't spend stay saved. Set a goal ("Flight 10,
  12 pp") and watch the bar fill. The full log is in Advancement (DCA 190); each form keeps its own ledger.
- **Range** – every power shows how far it reaches (ranged bands, close, perception, area size and where it's placed,
  movement per action), the Offense table has a Range column, and Strength shows what you lift and how far you
  throw a person or a car. 📏 Range (Forge, Play view, and the Rules tab) is a cheat sheet and calculator: pick a
  power and a distance to get the band and penalty (or the Extended Range that would reach it), area sizes,
  distance by rank, travel time at a speed rank, lifting and throwing (DCA 126, 129, 172-173).
- **Play view** (▶ Play on the Forge) – one screen for running a character at the table, made for phones: hero
  points, bruises and the damage track (type the damage rank of a hit and press Resist: the Toughness check is rolled
  and the bruise, dazed, staggered or incapacitated applied), fatigue and extra effort, conditions (vulnerable halves
  Dodge and Parry, impaired and disabled count on every check), tap-to-roll defenses, attacks and skills (results go
  to the Table), drain and pool trackers, companions, and an end-of-scene reset. It stays with the character.
- **Undo and history** – every change on the Forge can be undone and redone (↶ ↷, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y): edits, re-rolls,
  form switches, drains. 🕘 History keeps named versions of each character (one every time it is saved to the roster,
  or save one by hand) to restore later.
- **GM Screen** – the table runs itself. Every character in play on one board: players' characters live from their
  apps over the lobby (damage, conditions, hero points, pools, drains, initiative), plus villains and NPCs the GM
  runs here. Type a hit in plain words ("Burst Damage 10 hits Shadowshot and Spark", "Affliction 8 vs Fortitude on
  Spark") and each player's app rolls its own Dodge for half and resistance and applies the result; the GM's own
  characters roll here; everything goes to the Table feed. Initiative for everyone with turns and rounds, shown on
  every player's phone (a bar along the bottom: who's acting, who's next, and "Your turn" with what their conditions
  allow). **Next turn** ends the acting character's turn by the rules: a resistance check against each Affliction
  (each player's app rolls its own), Dazed from damage wearing off after their next turn, weakened traits coming back
  a point a round; **Delay** lets someone wait. ★ moments
  and a **spotlight** meter that names who hasn't had one; describe the scene and the **complications** it could bring
  into play come up, +1 hero point in one tap; **recurring villains** who learn from every defeat recorded in the
  World (points spent on the defense that kept failing, inside the PL limits) and work a **scheme clock** between
  sessions that leaves hints in the World for the Newsstand; and **End session** makes a comic-book cover recap and
  a few lines for the group chat (also saved in the World). Live play needs a table: open one for phones (below) or
  host one from the desktop app.
- **Join from any phone** – 📱 Open a table for phones (GM Screen or Table) shows a QR code and a link. Players scan
  it and they're in, in any browser, no install: they claim their character, roll on their phone, take hits and hero
  points from the GM Screen and see the battle map. The GM's page is the table (browser to browser over WebRTC via
  PeerJS), so keep it open while you play. Players can open the **Party** on the turn bar to see how their
  teammates stand. A phone that drops (it went to sleep) gets back in by itself. When a join fails, the Table says
  how far it got and what to try, and **Test my connection** checks this network. It uses the free public PeerJS
  server; under **Connection settings** a GM can set their own, and add a relay (TURN) server for strict networks
  (school, work, some phone carriers). Both travel in the table link.
- **Session in a Box** (GM Screen > 🧰 Prep) – press one button before game night and get a whole session: a villain
  (a recurring nemesis with an unfinished scheme first, then someone from the heroes' own backstories, else a new
  one) tuned in the Battle Room to the difficulty you pick, a minion squad, four scenes (hook, investigation with skill
  DCs, action, climax) with the spotlight going to the quiet players first, and a rumor for the newspaper. Put the
  villain on the board, set up the fight, or play a scene, each in one tap.
- **Rogues' gallery** (GM Screen > 🧰 Prep) – the enemies, rivals, exes and estranged mentors named in your players'
  backstories, ranked. "Make the villain" builds one as a legal character aimed at that hero: the main attack goes
  after their weakest defense, their defenses harden against the hero's best attack, with an Obsession complication
  and an origin that ties back to the story.
- **Battle map** (GM Screen > 🗺 Map) – a virtual tabletop. Keep as many maps as you like (new, rename, duplicate,
  switch), from 5 to 200 squares a side, with zoom and drag-to-scroll.
  - **Your own map picture**: upload one, say how many squares across it is, fine-tune the alignment, and the grid
    sits on top.
  - **🏠 Build**: 78 Monopoly-style pieces in seven groups. City (roads, crosswalks, sidewalks, parking, lamps,
    traffic lights, benches, bus stops), buildings (small houses to 60-floor towers, shops, diners, a church,
    a police station, a gas station), vehicles (cars, taxis, police cars, vans, trucks, buses, a tank, a
    helicopter), construction (a tower crane, scaffolding, an excavator, girders, cones, containers), nature and
    parks (trees in three sizes, oaks, palms, ponds, lakes, park lawns, fountains, swings), people (civilians,
    crowds, police, workers, paramedics, reporters, kids, dogs), and space and caves (stalagmites, crystals, lava,
    craters, consoles, power cores, escape pods). Drag them, stretch them, add floors, recolour, turn, copy.
  - **🧱 Paint**: walls, total and partial cover, difficult ground, water and hazards, square by square or in a box.
  - **Ground, weather and light**: grass, city streets, desert, snow, dirt, dungeon, cavern, moon, deep space or a
    starship deck; clear, rain, a thunderstorm, snow, a blizzard, fog, a sandstorm or ash; day, dusk or night,
    with a reminder of what each does at the table.
  - **Rules on the grid**: pick a token to see every distance with its range band and penalty, whether a wall
    blocks the line, and partial (−2) or total (−5) cover from walls, buildings, trees, cars and crates.
  - **💥 Templates**: burst, cone, line, cloud or cylinder, sized by Area rank. Drag one to move it, drag its tip
    to aim, or let it follow a token. "Hit everyone in it" sends an area hit (Dodge for half) through the GM Screen.
    Big creatures take 2×2 up to 6×6 squares, and tokens can be hidden from players.
  - **🧊 3D view**: the whole map in 3D, with the buildings, cars and trees as models, falling rain and snow,
    lightning, lit windows and street lamps at night, and tokens as standees with their portraits. Orbit around it,
    look from the top, or stand in any character's shoes and look around.
  - **Players see it live** on their phones (not hidden tokens), drag their own token, and open the 3D view too.
- **Battle Room** – a fight simulator on the DC Adventures combat rules. Throw in anyone (the open character, roster
  cards, any Bestiary creature, a whole Workshop minion squad), pick sides, and watch it play out turn by turn with a
  full log of every attack roll, resistance check, condition, hero point and extra effort. Pause, step, run a round, or
  run to the end; replay the same seed or rematch with new dice; run the match-up 100 times for the odds. **Will this fight wreck
  the party?** checks the heroes' odds over 100 fights and tunes the opposition (attacks and defenses up or down) to
  an easy win, a tense win, a coin flip, a deadly fight or a planned defeat. **📖 Make the comic** turns the finished
  fight into a comic page: the six biggest moments as panels with portraits, captions and SFX, to copy, download as a
  picture or share. Every fighter
  fights in a style that fits its build: brawlers close in and go all-out, skilled fighters feint and chain Takedowns,
  gear fighters and blasters keep their range and trade accuracy for effect, spellcasters rotate effects to the weak
  defense, mentalists turn controlled enemies on their friends, beasts lunge and grab, minions mob and break.
  Afflictions with all three degrees and recovery checks, area effects with Dodge for half, Multiattack, Impervious vs
  Penetrating, Immunity, Concealment, Healing, Regeneration, grabs, Interpose, Evasion and more.
- **Campaign journal** – on the Bio page: sessions played, what happened, who was met (added to "People in their life"
  or with their status updated), points earned and what they bought, and a rolled "between adventures" event from the
  life tables. Sessions join the life timeline as "In play" and go into every export.
- **Sessions** (World > 🗓 Sessions) – plan game night: the date (with a calendar file and an invite for the
  players), the scenes you have in mind linked to World pages, notes, and the Session in a Box plan, which the GM
  Screen keeps with the next session. **Plot threads** stay in view until they're tied up. Start a planned session
  and "Previously on…" goes to the table: the last recap, what changed in the World, the open threads. End session
  marks it played with its recap. Sessions travel in world keys.
- **World** – a wiki of the whole campaign, Obsidian-style. Every saved character is a page; every parent, sibling,
  mentor, rival, enemy, ally and ex named in their life is a page too (and opens as a full character); teams, cities and
  homelands are pages; journal sessions and recorded battles are events. Links are typed and run both ways (mentor /
  student, parent / child, member of / member, based in / base of...). Add factions, locations, items and events by
  hand, link anything to anything, draw the relationship graph, read the campaign timeline, and export the whole
  thing as a Markdown vault with [[wikilinks]] or a JSON vault that also carries the roster.
  **What happened** records history in one line ("these two became enemies", "she joined the Court", "he died",
  "they moved to Ravensport": 60-odd kinds across people, loves, factions, places, fates and things). Each one starts
  the new tie at that session, ends the tie it replaces (kept on the pages as "formerly"), marks a fate on the page,
  lands on both timelines and the campaign timeline, and can be undone. The graph has an "as of session" slider to
  wind the world back. Factions live on the character too (Bio > Factions: role, kind, since) and travel with the
  sheet and the exports. **Share the whole world as one key** (Vault page or the 🔑 button): pages, ties, moments
  and the roster in a `DCUW1.` string that players paste into the Forge's "Open a key" box and choose merge or
  replace.
- **Newsstand** (in the World) – front pages of your world's newspapers. Draft one from the campaign (the latest
  sessions, battles and recorded moments become the headlines, with reporter copy, bylines and a weather ear) or
  write one by hand; add pictures (upload, a roster portrait, or a free AI painting with a newsprint or comic
  treatment), adverts and classifieds (rolled from your factions and places, or your own), and pick a look:
  broadsheet, tabloid, EXTRA!, vintage gazette, comic panel, underground zine. Save it to the World (it sits on the
  campaign timeline, tied to the people in its stories), print it, or send it as a key or link that opens on any copy.
- **Handouts** (in the World) – props for the table, drafted from the World or written by hand: WANTED posters
  (portrait, aliases, crimes from what happened, last seen, a reward from their PL; Old West, noir bulletin, agency
  most-wanted, off-world bounty board), agency case files (mugshot, threat assessment, associates, stamps and
  [[redacted]] passages the GM can reveal), letters (handwritten, typed, telegram, memo, ransom note cut from
  magazines), phone screens of texts, and lab, police, medical or military reports. Saved as pages tied to the people
  in them, printed, or sent as a key or link.
- **Evidence board** (in the World) – the campaign as a detective's corkboard: pin people as Polaroids, factions
  and places as index cards, front pages as clippings and handouts as documents; the ties between them appear as
  red string, sticky notes hold the theories, and you can run your own string between anything. Drag everything;
  the layout lives in the vault and prints as a handout.
- **Life stories by hand** – every part of the Bio (story, family, people, timeline, sections, personality, inner
  life, GM hooks) can be edited, or written from a blank page ("Write your own" / "start blank").
- **Portraits** – every character gets a portrait on the case file, the Bio page and the World: built-in comic art
  drawn by the app (offline, instant) or an AI painting from a free, open-source service (one at a time, with the
  built-in art standing in while it paints), or a picture you upload. New portrait, edit or copy the prompt, delete.
  The portrait goes into the Excel character sheet's PORTRAIT box.
  AI paintings come from AI Horde by default (free, open source, volunteer GPUs; anonymous works, a free key from
  aihorde.net/register skips the queue) in four styles: comic book, semi-realistic, anime, painted. Pollinations.ai
  remains as the other painter.
- **Enemies** – "Add an enemy" on the Bio page rolls a full villain (nuisance, threat or nemesis) with a reason and a
  status, puts them in the character's life with a key so they open as a character, and on the sheet as a complication.
- **The Table** – a shared feed of dice. Every sheet has a "Roll" menu (initiative, attacks, resistance checks,
  skills, abilities) and the results land on the Table with the dice shown; a quick roller and chat live there too.
  Open a table for phones and players join by QR code or link from any browser (or host from the desktop app for
  players on your network): they get your world and roster, claim their own characters, and roll for them where
  everyone can see.
- **Backup** – "Back up everything" (Roster tab) saves roster, world, battle, notes, screen and settings to one file;
  "Restore" merges or replaces. The desktop app also mirrors everything to a folder on disk, with daily backups.
- **Advancement** – track the power points the GM awards (DCA 190): earned, spent and unspent, with a history log
  that records exactly what each point bought, tied to the journal session it came from.
- **Your data** – kept in the browser's IndexedDB (hundreds of MB, so saved portraits no longer fill it up), and
  mirrored to its small storage when it fits. **Sync with a cloud folder** (Roster > Backup) keeps everything in one
  file in Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive or iCloud Drive and merges it between computers, deletions included
  (Chrome, Edge, Opera and the desktop app; elsewhere use Back up / Restore).
- **Exports** – the text stat block, an Excel workbook and a printed dossier carry every alternate form and companion
  (and the pool and drain state); the .json character file carries everything.
- **House rules** (Rules tab) – PL limits and the point budget by the book, as warnings, or off; Weaken drains by how
  much the check missed or 1 point per degree; warnings for pool points over the limits. Rule problems show the book
  page where the data has one.
- **Roster** – save characters, teams and creatures in the browser; search, duplicate, delete.
  **🔗 Share as a link**: everyone in The Multiverse Hub, one world or folder, or the cards you selected, in one
  link (or a short key). Whoever opens it gets those characters added to their roster, in the same worlds and
  folders. Nothing is uploaded: everything is inside the link. The World's Vault page has a **🔗 Copy world link**
  for the whole campaign (pages, ties and roster) too.
  **Worlds** for a multiverse (Earth-1, Earth-2, a homebrew setting), with folders inside them ("Earth-2/Villains");
  **The Multiverse Hub** lists everyone you've made, in every world. Pick the world with the "Save to world" dropdown
  at the end of the Forge sheet (rolled, built from scratch, or a whole rolled team), or later: drag a card onto a
  world or use "Move to…", select several and move, export, delete or throw them in the Battle Room together; sort by
  name, PL, side, archetype or world; cards or a compact list. The world travels with the character in every export
  and key. The Forge keeps the characters you have open through a reload and only rolls when you press Roll.

- **Export** – your Excel character sheet filled in (plus a *Full Stat Block* tab), a whole roster or team to one
  workbook, JSON files, book-style text and print/PDF. **Import** brings characters back from DCUGen Excel sheets, .json
  files, share codes or links (Forge > Import, or drag files onto the page).
- **GM Tools** – dice with the book's coloured degrees-of-success chart, initiative with conditions, a combat
  calculator (hit and outcome odds, or roll it), the book's Damage Resistance Matrix, a GM screen with live hero point / Toughness /
  condition trackers, GM notes, and encounter, hideout and vehicle generators.
- **Rules** – searchable reference cards: actions, maneuvers, checks, combat, hazards, conditions, the DC,
  degrees and measurement tables, and every effect, extra, flaw, advantage and skill, with page numbers.

## Desktop app

`desktop/` wraps the same single file in an Electron app: your data in a folder on disk that survives updates, daily
backups, and the lobby server for hosting a table. Run it with `npm run desktop` from the repository root (any shell), or build installers with
`npm run desktop:dist`. See [desktop/README.md](desktop/README.md).

## Rules sources

Rules data lives in `data/` with page references (see `data/SCHEMA.md`): DC Adventures Hero's Handbook (effects,
modifiers, advantages, skills, equipment, constructs, animals, reference), Power Profiles (power themes), Martial
Arts (166 fighting styles), Gadget Guides (devices) and Heroes & Villains Vol. 1 (stat blocks used as tests).

## Development

Needs Node 18 or newer, nothing else.

```bash
npm test          # rules engine, generators, exports
npm run e2e       # builds DCUGen.html and drives it in a browser (Playwright; first time: npx playwright install chromium)
npm run build     # builds DCUGen.html (not committed: CI attaches it to every release)
npm run dev       # http://localhost:5178/src/app/index.html (live) and http://localhost:5178/ (built)
npm run validate  # check every catalog creature against the PL limits
```

- `src/engine/` – the rules engine: costs, PL limits, derived traits, character generator, workshop generators,
  power math, stat blocks, the .xlsx writer, the battle simulator (`battle.js`), the campaign journal (`journal.js`)
  and the world wiki model (`world.js`).
- `src/app/` – the web app.
- `desktop/` – the Electron app (main process, preload bridge, dependency-free WebSocket lobby relay).
- `tests/e2e/` – browser tests of the built app: roll, edit, save, share, every tab, the World, the GM Screen,
  the session planner, tables. CI runs them before every release. `E2E_NETWORK=1 npm run e2e` also runs a real
  phone table through the public PeerJS server.
- `data/changelog.json` – What's new in the app (the ? menu) and the top of each GitHub release's notes. Add an
  entry for each release.
- `tests/` – the engine reproduces the printed point costs of published Heroes & Villains characters, and
  thousands of random characters, items and creatures are checked against the rules.
