# Battle engine modules

`src/engine/battle.js` is a thin facade that re-exports the public names (the header comment there says what is and
is not simulated). The code is split by job:

- `util.js`: the per-turn random stream, the log, dice, small helpers, who is alive.
- `checks.js`: the one way to resolve a check: degrees, the natural 20 critical success, opposed checks with the tie rule, `rollCheck` for resistance and other checks against a DC. Every check except attack rolls and initiative goes through it. A check can carry a fortune hook (`fx`) that re-rolls it or fixes it at 20.
- `constants.js`: the round limit.
- `grid.js`: the 12 by 12 grid (`b.grid`), its scales (street 5 ft, block 30 ft, district 250 ft), the wall ring of an enclosed room, square names, and turning old saved zones into squares. `geometry.js`: distances in squares and feet, reach, range bands, a clear line, moves per turn, area templates. `path.js`: walking (around walls and cover), reachable squares, the best square to back off to. `placement.js`: teams on their edges and where an ambush puts fighters. `trace.js`: the optional `b.trace` record the grid tests read.
- `styles.js`: fighting styles, targeting tactics, `detectStyle`.
- `conditions.js`: Afflictions parsed into conditions, `activeConditions`, defenses, `canAct`.
- `combatant.js`: building and rebuilding a combatant from a character.
- `descriptors.js`: what an attack is made of (fire, cold, magic, bullets, psychic...). One table of descriptors with their words, family (physical or energy) and how common they are. An attack's descriptors are its power's own, else its array's or parent's, else read from its name and detail, plus the character's power source (a magician's spells are all magic), a device's technology and a Magic effect's magic. Also the Variable Descriptor groups and the plain-words lists for the Battle Room.
- `immunity.js`: every combat Immunity option read from the power's detail by the book's ranks (descriptor damage or effects, critical hits, interaction skills, entrapment, fear, sleep, life support, your own powers...): `immunityHit` (the one that stops an attack, with its Reflect and Redirect), `critImmune`, `interactionImmune`, `slipsHold`, `describeImmunities`. `insubstantial.js`: Insubstantial forms as fight state, what each form is immune to, `insubLoss` (no Strength, nothing physical when incorporeal). `insub-turn.js`: the free action to change form and flowing out of a hold. `variable-descriptor.js`: Variable Descriptor, switching an attack to a descriptor the target is not immune to.
- `odds.js`: hit and resistance odds, immunity (`isImmune`, `halfImmune`: both ask immunity.js), `expectedValue`, range and concealment.
- `plan.js`: the best attack against a target, the trade-off, and `choosePlan`.
- `turn.js`: `takeTurn` and the checks that can end a turn early. `turn-attack.js`: the attacking half of a turn.
- `movement.js`: `moveTo` (logs the squares and feet), `moveToward`.
- `attack-roll.js`: attack wording, the to-hit modifier and roll, the Defend roll, the hero point re-roll (and a critical turned into a normal hit by an Immunity to critical hits). `deflect.js`: Deflect, who can deflect for whom and the roll that raises the number to beat. `deflect-plan.js`: when a fighter takes the Defend action to deflect. `reflect.js`: the log line for a deflection and Reflect and Redirect, for Deflect and for an Immunity with Reflect.
- `build-report.js`: which of a fighter's powers, modifiers, advantages and skills the simulator uses and which it ignores, read from the shipped coverage list (`src/generated/battle-coverage.js`, made from `tools/battle-coverage/coverage.json` by `node tools/build.mjs data` or `node tools/battle-coverage/report.mjs`).
- `attacks.js`: `resolveAttack`, Interpose, Fast Grab, Takedown, area attacks.
- `attack-effects.js`: what a hit does (damage, Weaken, Affliction, Nullify), after Variable Descriptor, Insubstantial and Immunity have had their say (each says so in the log).
- `maneuvers.js`: feint, grab, escape, release. `support.js`: healing, hero points, Recover, Regeneration.
- `points.js`: who can pay for a re-roll (hero points, Luck) and the payment. `fortune.js`: hero points and Luck on a fighter's own checks (Improve Roll, the hooks `checkFx`, `resistFx`, `dodgeFx`, `recoveryFx`, `plainFx`) and Ultimate Effort. `luck.js`: Luck Control (grant, bestow, negate, force a worse re-roll), who may use it and what it does; `luck-attack.js`: when it is worth using on attack rolls. `fortune-kinds.js`: which checks an Ultimate Effort covers and which skills a Daze uses. `feat.js`: Heroic Feat (Interpose to save an ally, Power or All-out Attack for a finishing blow). `fortune-turn.js`: recovering from fatigue, Leadership and Inspire. `tricks.js`: the feint skill (Agile Feint, Startle) and Set-up. `daze.js`: Daze and when the AI uses it.
- `end-of-turn.js`: recovery checks and the weakened traits coming back.
- `battle-flow.js`: create, start, ambush, join, step, round, run to the end.
- `tactics.js`: team tactics, the small hooks the turn code calls (all neutral when a team has none). `tactics-list.js`: the list and `tacticOf`. `tactics-marks.js`: focus and lockdown targets, the VIP. `tactics-position.js`: the line, the guard, kiting (squares, not zones).
- `materials.js`: building materials, `toughnessOf` (the doubling rule) and the `COVER_KINDS` presets.
- `cover.js`: cover pieces (`b.cover`), their levels, who stands behind one, the attack penalty and Dodge bonus. `cover-damage.js`: hurting a piece (hole or destroy), shooting through cover, area attacks on scenery. `cover-plan.js`: when the AI shoots the cover itself.
- `support-actions.js`: Demoralize and Aid, and how they wear off. `support-plan.js`: when a fighter demoralizes or aids instead of attacking (its own judgement, and the Intimidate and Break and Aid the Champion tactics). `tactics-support.js`: who demoralizes, who the champion is, the flee chance. `tactics-withdraw.js`: the Fighting Withdrawal tactic (escape, rearguard, the hold-out win).
- `prone.js`: the prone state, the attack-check change from it (and Prone Fighting), falling prone. `stand.js`: standing up (the Stand action, DC 20 Acrobatics, Instant Up) and dropping prone on purpose.
- `items.js`: the items behind a fighter's attacks (held or worn, Toughness) and losing one. `maneuver-checks.js`: the attack check and the opposed check (`contest`) the maneuvers share. `trip.js`, `disarm.js`, `smash.js`: the three maneuvers and their odds. `plan-maneuvers.js`: when the AI picks one. `maneuver-run.js`: carrying the chosen one out.
- `move-by.js`: stepping away from melee threats before a shot (kiting) and the Move-by Action split (after the attack).
- `knockback.js`: the optional knockback rule, mass rank, flight and collisions. `tactics-cover.js`: the Take Cover tactic.
- `roles.js`: the roles a build suggests (healer, controller, debuffer, buffer, protector, striker, tank), each only a x1.15 weight on its kind of choice. `control-stop.js`: how completely a condition stops a fighter.
- `utility.js`: one utility for every way to spend a turn, in the expected degrees an attack uses (`expectedValue`). `utility-attack.js` values an attack (control adds the threat it removes: `utility-control.js`; Weaken is what the loss does to the team's next attacks or the enemy's threat: `utility-weaken.js`; `weaken-traits.js` reads and applies the trait). `utility-heal.js` (Healing, `support.js`) and `utility-buff.js` (gifts, `grants.js`) are scored beside the best attack in `utility.js`; `utility-protect.js` weighs Deflect (`deflect-plan.js`) and Aid to defense (`support-plan.js`). `grants.js`: Enhanced Trait, Protection and Regeneration lent to an ally with Affects Others. `utility-scale.js`: the exchange rates between those options, measured in the simulator (what a damage condition, a daze and a lost enemy turn are worth next to an attack); the turns each control condition costs are in `control-stop.js`.
- Flaws (Hero's Handbook p133-138, Martial Arts p94-98): `flaws.js` reads a power's flaws once into `atk.flaws` (null when none matter in a fight), shapes the attack (Inaccurate, Diminished Range) and holds the per-fight state (`c.flaw`: what is switched on, used, faded, done). `flaw-limits.js`: Limited and Dulled, a table of limits the engine knows the facts for (unknown limits keep the power working). `flaw-odds.js`: what the AI makes of a flaw (`flawFactor`, `flawRankLoss`, `flawUsable`), called from `expectedValue`. `flaw-gate.js`: what an attack must get past before it goes off (limit, chain, spent ranks and uses, Gallant, Unreliable, Check Required). `flaw-grab.js`: Grab-Based. `flaw-resist.js`: Sense-Dependent, Resistible and Inexact, on the target's side. `flaw-effects.js`: what a use costs afterwards (Fades, Tiring, Distracting, Side Effect, Prone, Push Over) and Feedback on a Snare. `flaw-turn.js`: Activation, Increased Action and Immobile, an Uncontrolled power going off, Concentration. Fights with no flawed attack never touch any of it.
- Extras (Hero's Handbook p125-133, Martial Arts p91-94): `extras.js` reads a power's extras once into `atk.ex` (null when none matter in a fight), the way `flaws.js` reads flaws. `resist-step.js`: the target's resistance check and what changes it (Potent and Stacking in the DC, Great Endurance, Second Chance, Partial Resistance, the minion's worst degree). `damage-result.js`: what a failed Damage check does (bruises, stagger, incapacitation, Sleep, Incurable, and dying in a lethal fight). `extra-after.js` writes down what a hit sets going (Secondary Effect, Increased Duration, Contagious, and Homing after a miss); `extra-run.js` carries it out on the later turns. `extra-bounce.js`: Indirect and Ricochet (Pinball, Recoverable, Bad Bounce), with `cover.js` ignoring cover for them. `extra-split.js`: Split. `attack-form.js`: the Attack extra on a Teleport. `extra-odds.js`: what the AI makes of the extras. `trap.js`: Triggered effects set on a square.
- Dying and death, only when the Lethal damage option is on (`b.options.lethal`, set in the Arena tab, tournaments and city war): `dying.js` (the dying condition, Fortitude DC 15 each round, death at three degrees of failure, Diehard, further damage), `debilitated.js` (abilities below -5 from Weaken), `stabilize.js` (a Treatment or Healing user steadying a dying ally). With the option off a fighter who would be dying is only incapacitated.
- Extra effort and fortune: `effort.js` (the benefits the fight uses: power rank, Strength, +2 on the attack check, an extra action, speed, an extra resistance check; Extraordinary Effort; once a turn). `counter.js`: countering effects (Instant Counter with a hero point, a readied counter) and the Improved, Diminishing and Overwhelming Counter extras. `ready.js`: the Ready action (a readied attack fires as a foe moves in; `movement.js` tells it after every move).
- Advantages in a fight: `favored.js` (Favored Foe, Favored Environment and the Arena's environments), `assessment.js`, `weapon-reactions.js` (Weapon Bind, Weapon Break, picking a disarmed weapon up again with Quick Draw), `trick.js` (Trick and Redirect).
- Power effects with their own action (Hero's Handbook p91-120): `power-options.js` gathers their turn options for `utility.js`, each scored in the units of an attack's expected degrees. Summon and Duplication: `summon.js` (reading the power: Multiple Minions, Horde, Active, Heroic, Sacrifice, Calling, Resistible; who has been called), `summon-build.js` (the minion character: rank x 15 points from the kind the power's words name, or the duplicate, scaled down below the full-copy rank), `summon-run.js` (calling the minions beside the summoner, the initiative order, Command as a move action, waiting for orders, minions vanishing with the summoner), `utility-summon.js` (what a minion is worth), `summon-sacrifice.js` (a hero point shifts a hit onto a minion). Create, Move Object and Element Control: `create.js` (a created object is a piece of cover with `created: true`: Toughness = rank, gone with its maker), `utility-create.js` (where a screen keeps enemy ranged fire off the most fighters), `power-attacks.js` (the attacks inside these effects, read like the sheet's own: dropped or thrown objects as Damage at the rank, a trap or seizure as a Snare of the rank, held to the power level limit). Illusion: `illusion.js` (the switch of looks and the opposed check, the Insight check afterwards, the illusion fading), `illusion-turn.js` (a fooled fighter attacking its teammate), `utility-illusion.js`. Environment: `environment.js` (impeding weather, light on darkness, extreme heat and cold every tenth round; the area follows the user), `utility-environment.js`. Changes of build (shapes, copies, forms): `mods.js` (temporary changes to a fighter's traits kept on it and put back on a rebuild: a Shapeshift form, a Mimic copy, absorbed energy), `form-score.js` (how good a build is against the foes now), `form-swap.js` (Alternate Form and Metamorph over the character's other builds), `shapeshift.js` (the menu of shapes), `mimic.js` (copying a foe's best attack), `form-turn.js` (which change is worth taking and when). Movement, body and Martial Arts effects: `movement-powers.js` (Super-Speed, Burrowing, Movement options, Extra Limbs read once; `path.js` lets a burrower or a Permeate user go straight through scenery), `immortality.js` (the dead return after 19 less the rank as a time rank, in rounds), `absorb.js` (Energy Absorption feeding a trait through `mods.js`, fading each turn), `extra-attacks-read.js` and `extra-attacks.js` (Extra Attacks: the extra attacks with the same power after the regular one). Transform: `transform.js` (a foe's weapon or a being with no Stamina). `feedback.js`: the Feedback flaw on a manifestation (a minion, a created object, an illusion).
- Martial arts (Martial Arts 3e): a fighter with `ch.styles` has `c.ma` (null otherwise), and nothing below runs for a fighter without it. `martial-read.js` reads the styles, their tier and the stances the fighter can assume with the check for each (the sheet's own `martial/stance.js`). `stance-fight.js` is the stance as fight state (`c.stance`): the Assume Stance check and its log line, the end conditions (`stanceUpkeep`, run after every turn), `tradeCap` (Offensive and Guarded widen the All-Out and Defensive Attack trade), `stanceBonus` (the grab and disarm bonuses). `stance-build.js` turns a stance into the changes `mods.js` applies, using the play view's `inStance` for attack, damage, initiative and skills so the two agree; `mods-martial.js` holds the new kinds of change (one attack's bonus and rank, active-only and resistance-only Dodge and Parry, stance conditions). `stance-plan.js` is the AI: each stance is weighed with its real numbers on the fighter, in expected degrees, against the stance held, after the chance the check fails (which leaves it in Neutral) and the move action a change costs; it also decides when an attack is disruptive. `stance-contest.js`: Identify Stance (a foe in a stance the fighter has not read is weighed as if in Neutral) and Disrupt Stance. `maneuver-table.js` and `maneuver-attacks.js` make the maneuvers the fighter bought into attack lines (basic ones from the table, the plain advanced attacks), `style-bonus.js` adds what the style gives its own attacks (its Close Combat skill, Student, Aggressive, Focused Strike, Nerve Strike, Flurry, Finesse Attack, Opposing Style) and what it does to the whole fighter (Listless, Slow, Defensive, Initiate), `style-extras.js` reads the style's extras and flaws, `style-turn.js` holds the round's short-lived effects (a maneuver's change to Parry and Dodge, Kata, Mobile, Fixed, Impractical, Bob and Weave), `ma-reactions.js` the free actions around an attack (Counterstrike, Defensive Throw, Follow-Up Strike, Sweeping Strike, Reversal, Damaging Escape, Quick Throw).
- `summaries.js`: status lines, battle text, many-run tallies, save and load.

The app is bundled by tools/build.mjs, which does not allow import cycles, so keep imports flowing one way: util,
constants and checks at the bottom, then styles, conditions, control-stop, utility-scale, weaken-traits, extras, debilitated, roles, grants, fortune-kinds, points, deflect, luck, descriptors, insubstantial, immunity, variable-descriptor, flaws, flaw-limits, flaw-odds, combatant, materials, cover, odds, fortune, tricks, feat, support-actions, prone, items, the tactics files, plan-maneuvers, utility-control, utility-weaken, utility-attack, plan, movement, maneuvers, support, fortune-turn, daze, utility-protect, support-plan, utility-heal, utility-buff, utility, deflect-plan, flaw-resist, attack-effects, flaw-effects, flaw-grab, flaw-gate,
luck-attack, attack-roll, extra-odds, resist-step, extra-after, damage-result, dying, favored, attack-form, effort, counter, extra-bounce, extra-split, weapon-reactions, trick, assessment, ready, trap, cover-damage, knockback, reflect, feedback, summon, summon-build, summon-run, summon-sacrifice, utility-summon, create, utility-create, power-attacks, illusion, utility-illusion, environment, utility-environment, mods-martial, mods, style-extras, martial-read, maneuver-table, maneuver-attacks, style-bonus, stance-build, form-score, form-swap, shapeshift, mimic, form-turn, transform, movement-powers, immortality, absorb, extra-attacks-read, power-options, attacks, extra-attacks, flaw-turn, stance-fight, stance-contest, style-turn, ma-reactions, stance-plan, turn-attack, insub-turn, illusion-turn, turn, end-of-turn, battle-flow and summaries on top. The grid files sit low: grid, then geometry (which needs conditions), path, trace and placement, all before cover and movement.
